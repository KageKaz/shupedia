Plato's friends have devised a plan for [[Socrates]] to escape his execution, but [[Socrates]] will have no part in it. He argues that a [[life]] that disobeys the law is not a good [[life]], and that only a good [[life]] is worth living. It is no good to return evil for evil, and as he has lived under the laws thus far, under the law he will continue to live.

> Let us fulfil the will of God and follow wither he leads
