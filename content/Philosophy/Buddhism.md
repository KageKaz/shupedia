# History
Originated in [[India]], spread to [[China]] by the [[Han Dynasty]].
# Beliefs
Four Noble Truths
+ [[life]] is [[suffering]]
+ we suffer because we crave
+ we cease suffering when we cease craving
+ the [[eightfold path]] leads to the cessation of [[suffering]] and craving
