Being: Everything that exists, but also everything that "is" something (i.e. being in the sense of a ball being red). Everything you can think of is part of being.
Unbeing cannot be thought of because thought and being are one.
Being is eternal because it cannot come from or turn into non-being. Even if things change from state to state, they will always continue to be.
