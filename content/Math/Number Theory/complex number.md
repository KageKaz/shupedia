> [!definition]
> A complex number is composed of a [[real number]] and an [[imaginary number]].

# forms
rectangular: $a+bi$
polar: $r(\cos (\theta)+\sin (\theta)i)$
exponential: $re^{i\theta}$
$$
\mid r\mid=\sqrt{ a^2+b^2 }
$$
$$
\theta=\tan^{-1}\left( \frac{b}{a} \right)
$$
$$
r\cos(\theta)+r\sin(\theta)i=a+bi
$$

# dividing
## by a [[real number]]
> [!example]
> $$\frac{2+3i}{4}=\frac{2}{4}+\frac{3}{4}i$$
## by a [[complex number]]
multiply [[numerator]] and [[denominator]] by the [[conjugate|complex conjugate]] of the [[denominator]]
