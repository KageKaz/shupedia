> [!definition]
> $$
_{n}C_{k}=\frac{\frac{n!}{(n-1)!}}{k!}=\frac{n!}{k!(n-k)!}=\binom{n}{k}
> $$

order doesn't matter
essentially, we take the formula for [[permutation|permutations]] and divide by the number of duplicates for every combination, or the ways to arrange the same $k$ things in $k$ spots, which is $k!$.
