A coordinate system consists of three basic elements:
1. Choice of origin
2. Choice of axes
	1. Choice of positive direction for each axis
3. Choice of unit [[vector|vectors]] at every point in space (to scale the coordinate system)

# Cartesian Coordinate System
Origin - choose a point
Axes - x,y,z, all at right angles
Positive direction - can choose 
Unit [[vector|vectors]]  ![[vector#^bcb3d1]]
To convert to polar:
$$r=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}$$
$$
\theta=\tan^{-1}\left( \frac{y}{x} \right)
$$
## complex coordinate system
In the complex coordinate system, the $y$ (vertical) axis is the imaginary part.
# Cylindrical Coordinate System (polar)
coordinates are $(r,\theta, z)$, radius, angle, and distance on z axis 
Origin - choose a point

To convert to Cartesian 
$$
x=r\cos \theta
$$
$$
y=r\sin \theta
$$
