> [!definition]
> The angle where the [[arc]] created is equal to the [[radius]] of a [[circle]].

The [[circumference]] of a circle is $2\pi r$, therefore a full revolution around a [[circle]], or $360$ degrees, is $2\pi$ radians.

radians are often used because they are defined naturally from [[geometry]], instead of just arbitrarily. This leads to cleaner, more elegant math.

> [!example]
> In [[degree|degrees]], the formula for [[arc]] length is $\frac{\theta}{360}2\pi r$, which actually just converts degrees to radians.
> In radians, it is just $\theta r$
