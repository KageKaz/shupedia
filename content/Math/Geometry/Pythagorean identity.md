In the [[unit circle]], $x^2+y^2=1$
We can define $\cos$ and $\sin$ in terms of $x$ and $y$ on the [[unit circle]], so
> [!definition]
> $$\sin^2(\theta)+\cos^2(\theta)=1$$

dividing by $\cos$:
$$\tan^2(\theta)+1=\sec^2(\theta)$$
dividing by $\sin$:
$$1+\cot^2(\theta)=\csc^2 (\theta)$$
