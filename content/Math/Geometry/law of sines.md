> [!definition]
> in **any** [[triangle]], the ratio of the [[sine]] of an angle to the opposite side length is equal for all three angles and sides.
> $$\frac{\sin(A)}{a}=\frac{\sin(B)}{b}=\frac{\sin(C)}{c}$$

This is useful for solving non-right [[triangle|triangles]] when you have an angle and its opposite side length and another angle or side length.
