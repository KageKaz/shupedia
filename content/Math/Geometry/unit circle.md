> [!definition]
> A [[circle]] of [[radius]] 1

By the [[Pythagorean theorem]], $x^2+y^2=1$

We can use the unit circle to define [[trigonometric function|trigonometric functions]] beyond $0^\circ$ to $90^\circ$. Instead of defining them with the sides of a right [[triangle]], we can simply say $\cos \theta=x$ and $\sin \theta=y$ because the [[hypotenuse]], which you normally divide by, is $1$.
