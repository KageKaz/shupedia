We know that from the [[multiplication rule]],![[multiplication rule#^1f9332]]
Thus, 
$$
\begin{align}
P(A\,\text{and}\,B)&=P(B)\cdot P(A|B)&&\text{just switching letters} \\
P(B)\cdot P(A|B)&=P(A)\cdot P(B|A)&&\text{set both equations equal to each other} \\
\end{align}
$$
> [!definition] 
> $$
> P(A|B)=\frac{P(B|A)\cdot P(A)}{P(B)}
> $$
