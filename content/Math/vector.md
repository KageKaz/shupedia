# operations
## Addition/Subtraction
To add vectors, we can put the tail of one on the head of the other, and the sum vector is at the end of that second vector. In other words, add the x and y components.
![[Pasted image 20240603233138.png|500]]
here, $\vec{A}+\vec{B}=\vec{C}$
To subtract, just flip the vector you are subtracting (add the negative)
The order of adding vectors does not matter;
## Multiplication/Division
multiplying/dividing by a [[scalar]] just multiplies the magnitude by that [[scalar]] 

# Unit Vectors
Unit vectors always have a magnitude of 1. 
$\hat{i}$ is in the positive x direction
$\hat{j}$ is in the positive y direction 
$\hat{k}$ is in the positive z direction ^bcb3d1

## Components
Vectors can be broken down into components.
![[Pasted image 20240603233817.png]]
We can represent the components in terms of unit vectors
$$\vec{A}=A_{x}\hat{i}+A_{y}\hat{j}$$
