> [!definition] 
> A probability distribution is the [[distribution]] of the [[probability]] of each possible outcome of a trial.

The probability distribution should always add to one.
> [!example] 
| 0             | 1             | 2             | 3             |     |
| ------------- | ------------- | ------------- | ------------- |
| $\frac{1}{8}$ | $\frac{3}{8}$ | $\frac{3}{8}$ | $\frac{1}{8}$ |
here is the probability distribution for the number of heads after 3 flips of  coin
