A z-score is![[z-score#^36a915]]
when dealing with means, 
$$
z=\frac{\bar{x}-\mu}{\frac{\sigma}{\sqrt{ n }}}
$$
We don't know what $\sigma$ is though, so we approximate with $S_{x}$. We call this $t$.
$$
t=\frac{\bar{x}-\mu}{\frac{S_{x}}{\sqrt{n}}}
$$
generally, 
$$
t=\frac{x-\mu}{S_{x}}
$$
t-tests are often used when the [[standard deviation]] of the [[sampling distribution]] is unknown. For example, when dealing with [[mean|means]] or [[regression line]] slopes. 

We can use a calculator or a table to find the [[probability]] of getting this value given the [[null hypothesis]] is true.
