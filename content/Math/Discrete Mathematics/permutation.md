> [!definition]
> $$
> _{n}P_{r}=\frac{n!}{(n-r)!}
$$

Order matters

> [!example]
> Suppose we are seeing how many ways we can have five people sit in five chairs. The first chair we have five options, the second we now have, four, etc. Thus, the number of permutations is $5!$. In this case, $n-r=0$. However, what if we only had three chairs? Then it would be $5\cdot4\cdot3$, or we divide $5!$ by $(n-r)!$, which is $2\cdot1$
