> [!definition]
> a rectangular arrangement of numbers into rows and columns.

# dimensions
rows $\times$ columns
> [!example]
> $3\times2$ matrix:
> $$
> B=\begin{bmatrix} -8&-4 \\
> 23&12 \\
> 18&10\\
> \end{bmatrix}
> $$
> $b_{2,1}=23$

# operations
## [[scalar]] 
> [!example]
> multiplication or division (multiply by [[reciprocal]]):
> $$
> 2B=\begin{bmatrix} 2\!\cdot\!-8&2\!\cdot\!-4 \\
> 2\!\cdot\!23&2\!\cdot\!12 \\
> 2\!\cdot\!18&2\!\cdot\!10\\
> \end{bmatrix}
> $$
