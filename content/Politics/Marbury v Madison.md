## Facts
Federalist [[John Adams]] loses to democratic republican [[Thomas Jefferson]]
Adams tried to appoint a bunch of judges before he left.
Not all these appointments were delivered, and when Jefferson comes into office, he tells his secretary of state [[James Madison]] to leave these appointments unfulfilled.
Marbury was one of those appointed judges that had not received the commission.  
Marbury sued Madison by a [[Writ of Mandamus]]

## Arguments
1. Does Marbury have the legal right to his commission?
	Yes - The appointment was done according to the [[US Constitution|constitution]] 
2. If yes, is the court-ordered [[Writ of Mandamus]] the proper legal means to get the commission?
	Yes
3. If yes, does the court have the authority to grant the [[Writ of Mandamus]]?
	**No**! 
	Article 13 of the [[Judiciary Act of 1789]] was declared unconstitutional. 
This case established [[judicial review]], making [[Supreme Court|SCOTUS]] the final interpreter of the [[US Constitution|constitution]]
