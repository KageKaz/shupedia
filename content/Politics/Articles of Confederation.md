First "constitution" of the US
Confederation - a form of government where several powers unite to form a central power.
# Context
After the [[American colonies]] declared independence from the [[British Empire]], each state needed a government and drafted its own constitution. In order to do business/foreign affairs, they needed a central government. Because the colonists still had a monarchy hangover, they wanted to keep power decentralized.

# Provisions
Power is centralized in the states
> Articles of Confederation and perpetual Union between the States of Newhampshire, Massachusetts-bay, Rhodeisland and Providence Plantations, Connecticut, New York, New Jersey, Pennsylvania, Delaware, Maryland, Virginia, North Carolina, South Carolina, and Georgia.

States are supreme
> **Each state retains its sovereignty, freedom and independence, and every Power, Jurisdiction and right, which is not by this confederation expressly delegated to the United States, in Congress assembled.**

Equal representation in [[US Congress|congress]]
>  In determining questions in the united states, in Congress assembled, each state shall have one vote.

Need a centralized government to do business with other nations
> No State, without the Consent of the united States, in congress assembled, shall send any embassy to, or receive any embassy from, or enter into any conferrence, agreement, alliance, or treaty, with any King prince or state

Army comes from the states
> but every state shall always keep up a well regulated and disciplined militia, sufficiently armed and accoutred, and shall provide and constantly have ready for use, in public stores, a due number of field pieces and tents, and a proper quantity of arms, ammunition, and camp equipage.

congress is given some mild powers 
+ arbiter between states over disputes

[[US Congress|congress]] can't do things like declaring war
> unless nine states assent to the same

Unanimity necessary for changing Articles of Confederation ^7adb46
> nor shall any alteration at any time hereafter be made in any of them, unless such alteration be agreed to in a congress of the united states, and be afterwards con-firmed by the legislatures of every state.

# Strengths
Because states have most of the power, the central government was very weak which avoided tyranny.

# Weaknesses
Federal government only had one [[legislative branch]], no [[executive branch]] or [[judicial branch]].
Required a unanimous decision by all 13 states to amend
[[US Congress|congress]] had no power to raise tax revenue, they had to ask the states for money, who were usually unwilling
+ [[US Congress|congress]] couldn't pay their bills
+ this included debt to war veterans, leading to [[Shays' Rebellion]]
+ [[US Congress|congress]] had no power to raise a national army in response
No national currency

As a result of all these weaknesses, a new [[US Constitution|constitution]] was drafted.
