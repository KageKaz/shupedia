Dred Scott was taken into free territory, so he sued for his freedom
The court ruled that because Dred Scott was not a citizen, he had no right to sue in federal court and the [[Missouri compromise]] was struck down as unconstitutional because it deprived people of property, opening up the entire nation to slavery
This is an example of [[judicial activism]] - Dred Scott's citizenship has no relation with the [[Missouri compromise]]
