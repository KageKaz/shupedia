# Historical Context
Created due to the failures of the [[Articles of Confederation]], thus it gives the central government much more power.
Created at the [[Constitutional Convention]] led by [[George Washington]]
Written by [[James Madison]]

# [[Enlightenment]] Influences
Because the framers feared mob rule, they instituted [[republicanism]] instead of a [[participatory democracy|direct democracy]].
Followed [[Baron De Montesquieu|Montesquieu]]'s [[separation of power]].

# Article 6 - National Supremacy
[[supremacy clause]]:
> This Constitution, and the laws of the United States which shall be made in pursuance thereof; and all treaties made, or which shall be made, under the authority of the United States, shall be the supreme law of the land; and the judges in every state shall be bound thereby, anything in the Constitution or laws of any State to the contrary notwithstanding.
