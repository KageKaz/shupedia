One of the [[Federalist Papers]]
## How long should judges be appointed?
Lifetime appointments by [[president]] during good behavior.
+ This makes it possible for judges to be impartial because they don't have to please an electorate.
+ This also encourages well qualified lawyers to become judges
## What power should the [[judicial branch]] have?
> [[judicial review]] - "To declare all acts contrary to the manifest tenor of the [[US Constitution|Constitution]] void"
* No unconstitutional law can be binding on a people governed by the [[US Constitution|constitution]]. 
* The [[judicial branch]] is not too powerful because it derives its power from the will of the people in the [[US Constitution|constitution]].
	> Nor does this conclusion by any means suppose a superiority of the judicial to the legislative power. It only supposes that the power of the people is superior to both; and that where the will of the legislature, declared in its statutes, stands in opposition to that of the people, declared in the Constitution, the judges ought to be governed by the latter rather than the former.
