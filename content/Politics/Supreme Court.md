Only court officially established by [[US Constitution]] 
* Established in Article III
Supreme Court Justices
+ Appointed by [[president]] 
+ Confirmed by [[Senate]] 
+ Hold lifetime appointments
Jurisdiction
+ Both [[original jurisdiction]] and [[appellate jurisdiction]] 
	+ scope of [[original jurisdiction]] is very narrow, majority are appeals
+ Has [[original jurisdiction]] in cases between two states or with an ambassador/public official ^0cf4ce

The [[Supreme Court]] has the power of [[judicial review]] ^a2eedaf

Each court ruling creates a precedent. 
+ Stare Decisis - Let the decision stand
+ precedents can be overturned, but usually only in monumental cases
+ Often [[president|presidents]] can appoint judges they think will overturn cases they don't like
+ Because decisions have far reaching effects, some question the legitimacy of the courts

### Judicial Philosophies
+ [[strict constructionism]]
+ [[loose constructionism]]
+ [[judicial activism]]
+ [[judicial restraint]]

### Checks
1. Passing laws that modify the impact of prior decisions 
2. Constitutional Amendments
3. Passing legislation that impacts the Court's jurisdiction
4. Judicial Appointments
5. Not enforcing the decision
