> [!definition]
> group-based activism by non-governmental [[interest groups]] which all work to impact political decision making makes it so no one interest can become dominant, ensuring compromise

By joining [[interest groups]], average citizens can have a larger collective impact on the government, amplifying their voice 
States interests can also be seen as interest groups

# pro
+ Federalist 10

# con
Hyperpluralism - pluralism gone mad. so many competing interests that [[gridlock]] ensues.
