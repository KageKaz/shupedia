no one branch of government holds all the authority, power is seperated.
However, even in their own spheres of power, branches do not have total authority because of [[checks and balances]]
# [[legislative branch]] 
+ [[Senate]] 
+ [[House of Representatives]] 
sole power to make laws
# [[executive branch]]
+ [[president]] 
+ [[federal bureaucracy|bureaucracy]]
sole power to enforce the law
# [[judicial branch]]
+ [[Supreme Court]] (and other federal courts)
sole power to interpret the [[US Constitution|constitutionality]] of laws 

Average citizens also have influences into each of these branches, through [[interest groups]], letters, litigation, etc.
