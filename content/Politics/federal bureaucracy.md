> [!info] The millions of people who are employed to carry out the responsibilities of the federal government

Falls under the executive branch
### Structure
Cabinet Departments
* 15 Departments
* [[president|president's]] cabinet are the heads of these departments
Agencies
* Agencies are divisions of departments
Independent regulatory commissions
+ Creating for the specific purpose of regulating society
Government Corporation
+ Hybrid between a government agency and a private business
+ Created when there are services the government wants to offer but the free-market is the best way to do it
## What they do
+ Write and enforce regulations
+ Issue fines for non-compliance
	+ This takes a lot of work, which can slow down the bureaucracy by a lot
+ Interact with [[US Congress|congress]]
	+ [[Iron Triangle]]

### How they do it 
Delegated discretionary authority - the power they have is delegated from the executive branch to enforce the law. 
They are required to enforce the law, but how they do it is up to them. 
This is often necessary because the executive branch does not have the expertise necessary to enforce the law 
Rule making authority - they make the specific rules for how a law is carried out

#### Civil Service Reform
Pendleton Civil Service Act created a merit-based system instead of the spoils system.
