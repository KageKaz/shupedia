Song rulers continued a revival of [[Confucianism]] from the [[Han Dynasty]]. This led to the emergence of Neo-[[Confucianism]], which had influences from [[Buddhism]] and [[Daoism]].

There was increasing growth of the imperial [[federal bureaucracy|bureaucracy]] 
## Women
Under [[Confucianism]], women were subordinates. They had restricted legal rights. [[foot binding]] was practiced. 

## Cultural Influence
### [[Korea]] 
[[Korea]] maintained a tributary relationship with [[China]], acknowledging their [[power]], which maintained a friendly relationship.
1. [[Korea]] used a similar civil service exam to staff the [[federal bureaucracy|bureaucracy]].
2. Korea adopted [[Confucianism|Confucian]] principles which organized their family structures, going even further than China in marginalizing the role of women.
### [[Vietnam]]
[[Vietnam]] had a similar relationship that [[Korea]] did. A difference was that women were viewed higher.
### Heian [[Japan]] 
Because [[Japan]] and [[China]] are separated by ocean, any cultural traits adopted were voluntary. They copied the [[federal bureaucracy|imperial bureaucracy]], [[Buddhism]], and writing [[system]].
