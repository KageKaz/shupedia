Called in 1787 to fix the weaknesses of the [[Articles of Confederation]], but quickly become an effort to draft an entirely new constitution.

![[Articles of Confederation#^7adb46]]
It was pretty much impossible to change the [[Articles of Confederation]]. To fix this, [[US Constitution#Article 5|Article 5]] of the [[US Constitution|constitution]] proposed an easier process
+ Either [[US Congress|congress]]  or special state conventions can propose an amendment
	+ Two-thirds vote needed to propose
+ Proposed amendments must be ratified or accepted by three-quarters of the state [[legislative branch|legislatures]] or state ratifying conventions.
