Farmers had gone into debt during the war, and could not pay off the debt due to inflation and [[tax|taxes]].
Farmers petitioned for relief, but the government refused
This angered the farmers.
Daniel Shays raised a 1000 farmer militia, who raided an arsenal and armed themselves.
The rebellion was crushed by the [[Massachusetts]] state militia. Even though the conflict was resolved, it exposed the lack of a central leader and national militia. Additionally, the state of the economy meant that more rebellions were likely.
