$$
\vec{F}=q(\vec{v}\times \vec{B})
$$
$$
F=qvB\sin \theta
$$
deriving in terms of wire length and [[current]].
$$
F=q\left( \frac{l}{t} \right)B\sin \theta
$$
$$
F=\frac{q}{t}lB\sin \theta
$$or
$$
\vec{F}=I(\vec{l}\times \vec{B})
$$

Because of how the [[magnetic field]] is generated, two parallel wires with [[current]] in the same direction attract, and wires with [[current]] in opposite directs repel.

When we move a charge through a [[magnetic field]], you generate a force on them, inducing a [[current]]. This is how electric generators work.
