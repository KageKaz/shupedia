> [!definition]
> a wave is an oscillation from [[equilibrium]] 

transverse waves occur when the particles move perpendicular to the direction of the wave
longitudinal waves occur when particles move parallel to the direction of the wave
![[Pasted image 20240422201751.png]]

![[Pasted image 20240422194853.png]]
every time you go one wavelength, you are back where you started. therefore, for a wave that can be defined as a [[sinusoidal function]]:
$$
\text{position}=A\cos\left( \frac{2\pi}{\lambda} t \right)
$$
period is the time for one wavelength
the number of wavelengths per unit of time is frequency
$$
\text{frequency }(f)=\frac{1}{\text{period }(T)}
$$
